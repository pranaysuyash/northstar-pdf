## 3. Wide set — full divergent pool (verbatim, all branches)

### 3.1 Branch: hardware engineer (`src: hardware engineer`)

1. **Load-time POST screen** — Render the synchronous main-actor parse at document open as a visible per-stage timing readout, BIOS-POST style, so the known latency cost is disclosed hardware-style instead of hidden. *Rationale: converts the last headline perf defect from a launch blocker into an honesty surface; cohort-scale form PDFs sit far under the 250MB budget anyway, and the readout doubles as the timing-budget instrument for the eventual async fix.*
2. **Ship with a datasheet** — Publish a validated operating envelope (file size, page count, field classes, engine coverage) in the listing the way components ship abs-max ratings. *Rationale: hardware never holds the ramp for worst-case corners; the 250MB parse and parity-red corners fall outside a published envelope, so the product can go on sale honestly while gates close inside it.*
3. **Cohort-as-fixture campaign** — Re-scope the 0/38 acceptance fixtures to the exact recurring public forms the first cohort actually files (W-9, I-9, state sales tax) so gate-green literally means cohort-ready. *Rationale: captures real traffic instead of synthetic benchmarks; collapses the evidence blocker and the first-customer blocker into one work item, and per-form green results become verifiable purchase claims.*
4. **Burn-in service seat** — Pre-run each cohort member's flagship recurring form through the full human-review pipeline before delivering the license, shipping golden-unit calibration with every seat. *Rationale: at cohort scale (tens, not thousands) vendor-side pre-burn is feasible, gives a pre-validated first run without touching the capability freeze, and manufactures the trust a zero-user product cannot yet earn organically.*
5. **Secure-boot licensing** — Wire commerce as a hosted payment link plus a signed offline entitlement the app verifies locally like a firmware signature — no license server, no egress. *Rationale: smallest legal commerce surface that satisfies the zero-network doctrine, gets the first $79 flowing without in-app purchase plumbing, and turns renewals into a cryptographic re-sign rather than a server dependency.*
6. **Public burn-in errata board** — Publish the acceptance campaign live — fixture-by-fixture red/green with timestamps — as a hardware-style validation-log and errata page. *Rationale: for an evidence-first zero-egress product, visible honest redness is the differentiator; the log doubles as the pre-purchase trust asset and the VoC magnet a no-user product otherwise lacks.*

### 3.2 Branch: biology (`src: biology`)

1. **Niche partitioning (Darwin's finches)** — Scope the first paid SKU to the sub-50MB recurring-form niche where the current parse already performs, publishing large-document support as a future adaptation — decoupling first revenue from the 250MB main-actor rewrite. *Rationale: removes the headline perf issue from the launch critical path by contracting the addressable niche to where the existing engine is already fast; a market/spec contraction consistent with the capability freeze and single-document-first doctrine, not an open-time triage of individual documents.*
2. **Apoptotic fixture culling** — Any acceptance fixture that fails human visual review twice with the same defect signature undergoes programmed death — retired into a published known-limitations ledger — shrinking the 0/38 gate to its viable set. *Rationale: converts an all-or-nothing wall into a shrinking denominator so the gate can reach green on viable fixtures while honestly recording every culled one, preserving the no-silent-conversions and honest-claims doctrine.*
3. **Horizontal gene transfer** — Graft pre-existing open-source PDF conformance corpora (veraPDF, pdf.js test suites) onto the acceptance gate as donor fixtures instead of hand-authoring all 38 from scratch. *Rationale: imports already-validated test material so the human gate starts from inherited green mass rather than zero, compressing the fixtures timeline without fabricating any passed:true evidence.*
4. **VDJ recombination** — Assemble a "cohort edition" build by recombining the existing detector, writer, and validator modules around the three most common SMB form families — diversity at the module joints, generality never. *Rationale: a narrow SKU recombinated from existing pipeline parts reaches sellable parity far faster than the general product, requires no new capabilities (freeze respected), and gives the first cohort a product that provably matches their actual forms.*
5. **Quorum sensing** — Open a refundable $79 founder-deposit lane where every deposit must carry one real recurring form as its autoinducer, and the perf fix plus commerce wiring are only triggered once deposit density crosses a set threshold (e.g., 25). *Rationale: turns zero users into a density signal that gates engineering spend; the submitted forms become cohort evidence automatically, so demand is proven with money and artifacts before the remaining red gates are worked.*
6. **Microbiome seeding** — Incubate distribution inside an adjacent bookkeeping habitat — a QBO-adjacent utility, bookkeeper newsletter, or practitioner forum — shipping as a symbiotic fill-companion rather than selling direct. *Rationale: borrows a host audience that already digests acquisition for regulated SMB bookkeepers, sidestepping the cold-start GTM problem without interviews, paid ads, or a new parallel product.*

### 3.3 Branch: markets (`src: markets`)

1. **Fixture auction** — Take refundable $79 deposits that bid which real forms get certified next from the pending gate queue, so demand prices the 0/38 backlog instead of internal sequencing. *Rationale: turns the pending visual-acceptance fixtures from an internal cost into a priced order book; deposits pre-qualify real paying buyers and rank the gate work by willingness-to-pay without touching the price level.*

4. **Maker inventory seeding** — Buy out one real bookkeeper's current form backlog and run it through the pipeline as the market-maker's opening inventory. *Rationale: a market-maker must hold inventory before quoting; acquiring a real recurring-form backlog instantly stocks the gate with authentic workload, produces fixture passes on genuinely sold-for forms, and creates the first served customer.*

6. **Exchange listing** — Pursue listing on the venues where bookkeepers already procure tools (state CPA societies, QuickBooks ProAdvisor/App marketplaces, association vendor catalogs) with the fixture gate ledger published as listing disclosure. *Rationale: an exchange listing is a trust shortcut: the cohort shops inside regulated professional channels, and disclosing the live gate status (pending, not claimed-passed) converts the evidence pipeline itself into the listing standard that gets the product distributed.*

### 7.2 Branch 2 — Refund-escrowed presale with a demand-priced gate backlog (R3-25; variants: R3-29, R3-14, R3-19, R3-13, R3-11; delivery mechanism: R3-05)

**Sketch (verbatim).** The mechanism stays small because the heavy parts already exist: the repo's gate manifest (the 38 named form classes RG-135 measures) is exported as a static, signed `form_class_ledger.json` rendered by one static page showing green/pending status and fixture counts per class. A founder deposit is a single $79 payment-link charge whose checkout includes a free-text "name your recurring form" field plus an optional consent checkbox to contribute a redacted copy of that form; each charge maps to exactly one nomination. Deposits aggregate into a public per-class order book, and each engineering cycle certifies the highest-density pending class, with weekly ledger updates including slip history. When the nominated class passes the human visual and parity gates, the deposit converts — the checkout terms stated exactly this condition, so there is no silent conversion — and a signed, offline-verifiable entitlement (reusing the existing signed-receipts pipeline) unlocks that class in the app. If the class never passes, the refund is unconditional and processed manually through the payment processor, and the failure is published on the ledger itself. Total new infrastructure is one static page, one payment link, and one JSON reconciliation step — no commerce stack, no license server, and zero egress from the app.

**Load-bearing risk (verbatim).** Distribution: with zero users and no channel, the order book can sit at zero density for months, and an empty ledger actively undermines the trust story it was built to tell — a refundable-money ask is harder to give a stranger than a free download, not easier. A secondary structural hazard is that "escrow" is fictional here: cards are charged then refunded, so refund latency, processing fees, and chargeback exposure exist and must be owned in the terms rather than glossed over.

**First concrete step (verbatim).** Export the existing RG-135 gate manifest into `docs/public/form_class_ledger.json` and render it as a single static `ledger.html` (status, fixture count, and nomination link per class), then create one Stripe payment link at $79 labeled "Founder deposit — refundable until your named form class passes" plus a one-page refund-terms markdown. All three artifacts are derivable from current repo state and committable in a day with no runtime dependencies.

**Children (verbatim):**
1. **Dated delivery futures with slip-linked pricing**: each pending class shows a projected certify-by date, founder deposits get a discount that decays on a published schedule, and every slipped date is logged on the ledger so the price decay is provably tied to honest pace — late delivery visibly degrades the deal, not just the promise. *Rationale: makes delivery risk legible and self-punishing; converts the biggest buyer objection ("when?") into a transparent artifact and turns the slip history itself into credibility content.*
2. **Zero-cash quorum via card authorizations**: nominations bind as $0-authorization holds (or $1 auths that are immediately voided) instead of escrowed charges, and the first class to hit quorum density charges all its members at once on conversion; nothing is ever held, so there are no refunds to process and no chargeback exposure. *Rationale: removes the payment-ops Achilles heel entirely and makes quorum a pure intent signal; the weaker per-nomination commitment is absorbed by raising the quorum threshold.*
3. **Consent-gated fixture contributions as queue priority**: a second opt-in action in the deposit flow lets contributors submit a redacted copy of their form (strip field values, keep geometry and labels mechanically) and move up one certification tier per accepted contribution. *Rationale: the red gates are 0/38 partly because there is no fixture corpus; this makes deposits the corpus-manufacturing engine they claim to be while preserving zero-egress by construction through mechanical redaction plus explicit consent.*
4. **Signed offline entitlement as the delivery certificate**: on class pass, each quorum member receives an Ed25519-signed entitlement whose payload is just {form_class, pass_date, gate_receipt_hash}, verified against a public key embedded in the app, with the receipt hash pointing into the existing signed-receipts chain. *Rationale: completes the loop with no license server and reuses the strongest existing asset — signed receipts — so any third party can independently verify that an entitlement corresponds to a genuinely green gate.*
5. **Sell the ledger, not the app**: the distribution wedge is the ledger page itself, posted to bookkeeper and tax-pro communities as "the top-voted form gets certified next, money back if we fail", where anyone can nominate and vote for free before any money changes hands. *Rationale: directly attacks the named open distribution risk — a free vote is a shareable, zero-friction ask that builds the audience and demand data first, so deposit conversion happens on a queue with observed density instead of from a cold audience.*

### 7.3 Branch 3 — Parity clearing via netting (R3-15)

**Sketch (verbatim).** Treat the red parity gate as a clearing house: run the existing dual/triple-engine cross-check (MultiEngineValidator.reduce over EngineObservations, plus PDFImpactValidator/QPDFValidator export checks) across every fixture in Tests/fixtures and benchmark/datasets (checkbox/radio/choice), reducing each fixture to a ConformanceReport exactly as MultiEngineValidator already does. A thin settlement layer then nets all fixtures whose reports are exact-agreements (empty discrepancies array) into a single ParitySettlementReport artifact stating "N fields exact-agree across engines, attested per fixture ID", while any fixture with one or more discrepancies is minted into the residual dispute set M and routed to the existing reviewed-candidate queue for human disposition. Nothing is silently accepted: agreements are attested with per-fixture evidence pointers, disputes are surfaced individually, and human judgment is preserved for exactly the judgment calls. This changes the unit of human attestation from per-fixture to per-settlement (1 settlement review + M dispute reviews instead of N reviews) — that is its concrete delta from the prior-round conveyor (flow control), rework lane (failure routing), and VerificationState contract (state honesty), which all still compose with it. Because it is a pure local reduction over already-produced EngineObservations, zero-egress is untouched, and because the gate (D-055) still consumes one signed evidence artifact, gate authority stays concentrated — only the artifact's shape changes. The red gate can then flip honestly once disputes are dispositioned or M itself is small enough that fixing the underlying engine divergences is tractable work rather than an undifferentiated 0/38 wall.

**Load-bearing risk (verbatim).** The red gate's actual failure mode is unmeasured: if 0/38 redness is caused by missing fixtures, unexecuted harness runs, or attestation plumbing rather than genuine engine disagreement, netting settles an empty set and adds machinery with zero payoff. Netting shrinks human load only in proportion to the agreement ratio — if engines disagree broadly, M approaches N and the only honest gain is per-fixture accounting, not bottleneck relief.

**First concrete step (verbatim; harness path verified 2026-09-17).** Instrument `Sources/PDFExperimentParityHarness/main.swift` (or calibration-gate.sh) to emit per-fixture ConformanceReport outcomes over the full corpus and diff them against `benchmark/results/preflight-parity-2026-08-25/parity-report.json`, producing a one-page classification: fixtures exact-agree vs dispute vs never-run. Only if exact-agreements dominate does the settlement reducer (ParitySettlementReport, alongside `Sources/PDFEditorCore/MultiEngineValidator.swift` and `AcceptedVarianceRegistry.swift`) get built.

**Children (verbatim):**
1. **Make the settlement report a first-class D-055 gate input**: extend the benchmark/results gate report schema so the parity gate accepts either per-fixture confirmations or a settlement artifact with per-fixture evidence pointers. *Rationale: preserves concentrated gate authority and honest evidence claims while changing only what the gate reads — the gate stays one artifact, one decision point, no parallel gate route.*
2. **Tier the dispute set by discrepancy class** (page-count, text-presence, field-count, geometry variance) so each dispute routes to a matching AcceptedVarianceRegistry disposition or an engine-fix task. *Rationale: turns disputes into a monotone-decreasing classified backlog: every settlement round shows M shrinking as variances are accepted or bugs fixed, making gate progress measurable instead of binary.*
3. **Extend netting to the second red gate** — the human visual confirmation gate at 0/38 fixtures — by marking exact-agreeing fixtures as "engine-attested, spot-check" with a sampled k-of-N eyeball verification. *Rationale: the two gates block launch together; settlement that only touches one leaves the other as the bottleneck, and sampling keeps a genuine human judgment hook so the claim remains honest.*
4. **Net per fixture-family** (checkbox, radio, choice, Tests/fixtures corpus) so a settlement green can go family-by-family rather than all-or-nothing. *Rationale: one family's disputes stop holding up attestation of clean families; the gate can flip partially and honestly, giving incremental launch-signal and preventing a single pathological fixture from freezing all progress.*
5. **Cache per-fixture EngineObservations keyed by fixture-hash** so subsequent settlement runs re-net only changed fixtures and regressions appear as newly un-netted entries. *Rationale: makes settlement cheap enough to run on every CI cycle and turns the residual dispute set into a regression detector — any engine change that un-settles a previously agreed fixture is surfaced as a diff, not lost in aggregate ratios.*
