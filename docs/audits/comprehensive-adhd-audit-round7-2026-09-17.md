## 1. Branch outputs (verbatim)

### Frame: Improviser
1. **The Pocket** — during flow the ledger records tempo, not notes: a coarse outline of when and how long you played, never what you played.
2. **Liner notes** — closing an un-recorded set obliges a hand-written paragraph of memoir, filed as a distinct genre: testimony, not transcript.
3. **The Room Witness** — co-present collaborators co-sign an "I was in the room" attestation for a dark stretch.
4. **Chalk privileges** — work made in the dark renders readably but cannot be quoted, cited, exported, or diffed until deliberately brought into ink.
5. **The Call** — you may only enter un-recorded play by calling the tune; the call and the close are the sole atomic ledger entries for the session.
6. **Certified absence** — on surfacing, the ledger stamps the exact boundary of its own ignorance — "knowledge ends here" — and swears to it like any other fact.

### Frame: Diary keeper
1. **Burn tombstones** — the ledger's only entry for private writing is a notarized record that something was destroyed.
2. **Ciphertext ledger** — run the mutation-proof machinery on encrypted blobs: provable when/how often/whom, structurally incapable of knowing what.
3. **The sovereign margin** — each oathed document carries a marginal zone explicitly declared not-under-oath.
4. **Pre-registered silence** — sealed entries notarized with a hash and a release date, unopenable early even by future-you.
5. **Documents born adult** — promoting a private thought into a public record creates a first entry stamped "pre-history does not exist."
6. **Masked attribution** — private writing enters the ledger under a rotating pseudonym the keeper alone can map.

### Frame: Zen gardener
1. **Water-writing board** — compose on an evaporating surface; survival requires an explicit transcription act; the ledger records the handoff, never the making.
2. **Quorum-pooled attribution** — the ledger proves what changed and that someone changed it, while the who dissolves into "the table."
3. **Rake marks** — unrecorded work emits attested, content-free process telemetry (cadence, tempo, attention sweep).
4. **One-take seal** — a speed mode performed in a single unbroken gesture; the ledger testifies to absence: "performed, not edited."
5. **Pinch of sand** — witnesses receive non-reconstructive seals of co-presence with the making.
6. **Attested blindness** — a first-class entry type that records its own looking-away: "a session occurred, duration D, contents never seen."

### Frame: Playground designer
1. **Chalk-line fields** — entering play draws exactly two ledger entries — field opened, field closed — and the ledger agrees to see nothing in between.
2. **The sealed blob** — play time commits as an opaque, cryptographically sealed mass: who, when, how long; contents unreadable even to the player.
3. **The game did it** — during play, authorship collapses to one communal actor; anything carried back out wears a permanent degraded-provenance flag.
4. **Decay, not deletion** — play materials fade on a visible clock; the moment of full fade is the only ledger line.
5. **Pick-up-able laws** — in play, the document's own rules become loose parts; leaving play means rebuilding the rule stack before the ledger reopens.
6. **Zero-state instruments** — play tools carry no progress bars, no unsaved-changes guilt; nothing-counts as an interface property.

### Frame: Demolition & salvage
1. **Boundary survey** — a no-ledger session owes the archive only what it looked like before and after; the perimeter is attested, the interior never was.
2. **Mass-balance wreck report** — an un-recorded session closes with tonnage, not content: N items entered, M adopted, K vaporized.
3. **Containment envelope** — un-attributed edits sealed into one cryptographically closed unit, destroyed wholesale.
4. **Salvage provenance stamp** — anything kept re-enters tagged "recovered from unattested demolition."
5. **Blast permit** — the owner declares a region and duration; the ledger records that a permit existed and nothing of what happened inside it.
6. **Chalk-grade writes** — material produced in flow mode is structurally ephemeral, self-cancelling on a timer.

## 3. Deepened branches (focus-agent output; three passes)

### The session-boundary contract (deepens R7-18 attested blindness + R7-29 blast permit + family) ★
**Sketch:** A first-class `blind_session` ledger entry: entering dark mode with a pre-declared region R suppresses all durable content capture for ops touching R — no op records, no receipt digests, no undo snapshots — while ops outside R append normally. On close, ONE tombstone appends: {region, started/ended, in-region op COUNT only, resumption_digest = whole-document hash at close}, and the next op chains off the tombstone — the hash chain stays unbroken even though op contents are absent. Trial balance reports an honest structured verdict ("prefix verified to op K; replay impossible across dark span; suffix anchor-verified from resumption_digest; export bytes are the sole authority for the span"), and R6-14's coverage map gains a deliberate terra incognita row. D-067 gains a new evidence tier — **"asserted-by-mechanism"** — for the negative claim "contents never seen," because the only honest oath is that the recording paths were structurally closed. Three content dispositions are named: **chalk** (discard entirely; determinism trivially preserved), **seal** (commit the dark diff as one opaque snapshot-replayable entry), **salvage** (promote individual items as fresh entries tagged provenance=unattested-session, which D-078 binds to their own post-digest so the agent loop never sees dark digests).
**Load-bearing risks (two, both structural):** (1) suppression must be enforced at the engine data-path layer — any leak (execution-receipt digests, undo snapshots, autosave/crash journals, extraction caches) turns the tombstone from an honest oath into a false one; (2) a cheaply, silently invocable blind mode is the perfect tampering cover — invocation must itself be a witnessed, gated ledger act (the Call inside the tombstone: who opened, when, region declared).
**First concrete steps:** run the **content-sink census** — enumerate every persistence path that can hold document bytes (autosave, NSUndoManager, search index, snapshot caches, QuickLook), consolidate behind one registered ContentSink protocol, and write the chalk-disposition integration test (open dark → mutate → close-as-chalk → assert zero post-state bytes across every registered sink). Simultaneously extend R5-14's trial balance with the two dark-span replay semantics (skip for chalk, snapshot-load for seal).
**Children:** tombstone-as-replay-anchor (resumption digest keeps everything before/after fully verifiable) · the "asserted-by-mechanism" D-067 tier · witnessed invocation (the Call recorded inside the tombstone) · agent dark-span claim ceiling + agent blast permits (plans run on dark documents but claims intersecting a dark span cap at advisory "unknown, downstream of dark span") · counterparty export vocabulary (a machine-checkable dark-span attestation block, distinct from redaction) · silent-recorder CI contract (nightly test: dark session → close as chalk → grep all registered sinks for leakage) · crash semantics (unclosed dark span at launch auto-resolves to chalk with a session-abnormal-close tombstone — the failure mode is defined).

### Liner notes (deepens R7-02) ★
**Sketch:** A distinct append-only entry type `SessionLinerNote` whose genre is a literal ("linerNote"), carrying signed text, `coversDarkSpanID`, duration, writtenAt. On closing a dark span, the nudge scales: a 14-second span gets a one-tap "nothing happened worth noting" (which records a one-word honest testimony); a 14-hour span opens a short sheet with the "testimony, not transcript" framing. Skip is itself recorded (`linerNoteDeclined`) — either testimony exists or the absence of testimony is recorded, never a silent hole. "A feeling is not a fact" is enforced by the type system: every downstream consumer (export, diff, agent binding, receipts) must explicitly handle the type, each with one rule — render as clearly-marked testimony, never include in evidence packs, never diff as fact. Signature reuses ReceiptSigning with distinct meaning: "human-authored, subjective."
**Load-bearing risk:** genre erosion at the consumer boundary — the note lives in the same ledger file as receipts; one lazy consumer that "conveniently" includes it converts testimony into transcript with zero errors. Mitigation: test-enforced, not convention-enforced — a parity test asserting no consumer path mixes the types.
**First concrete step:** `Sources/PDFEditorCore/SessionLinerNote.swift` with the genre-literal Codable type + a decode-parity test asserting (a) ExecutionReceipt never decodes containing liner-note data, (b) liner notes are excluded from agent context packing and receipt-derived evidence — schema and enforcement before any UI.
**Children:** proportional nudge scale (duration-scaled prompts) · paired entries keyed by darkSpanID (the machine's looking-away + the human's account, rendered together — the two-sided record) · genre-legible export (distinct register, italic, fixed header — the genre marker leaves the app) · decline telemetry that stays honest (counts only; never auto-generated prose standing in for a missing note) · NorthstarHelpView genre explainer (naming the two record genres — receipt vs liner note — as the product's trust vocabulary).

### The honesty-pair composition note (R6-14 + R5-14 + this round)
The dark session composes with the two prior honesty features into a complete contract: **trial balance** (R5-14) proves global replay integrity; **terrae incognitae** (R6-14) maps the spatial extent of testimony — gaining a deliberate incognita row per dark span; **attested blindness** (R7) is the entry type that makes deliberate gaps honest. Three features, one oath: the ledger testifies, confesses, or swears silence — never gossips.

## 5. Provocation

The dark session gives the oath a second clause. Six rounds built a tool whose answer is always a receipt; this round built the tool's capacity for *sworn silence*. Two directions open: **(a) the spectrum question** — is honesty binary (oathed/dark) or graded (a dial from full testimony to certified absence to sealed opacity to chalk)? Design the dial. **(b) the growth question** — what does the *human* owe the record over years: R4-12's dormancy rehearsal and R7-02's liner notes are the first two entries in what looks like a genre nobody has named — the document's autobiography. Is the real product the paper trail, or the book it writes about you?
